# Terms of use

Printopoly is a free hobby project. What you make and print is yours, and at your own cost.

## Free, no guarantee

You only pay the print shop or your own ink and paper. We do our best to make the PDFs correct, but we don't guarantee that a print fits exactly or that colours come out as on your screen. So print the test sheet before you order everything.

As far as the law allows, we are not liable for damage from Printopoly, such as a wrongly printed board or a lost edition.

## Limits

Photos can be up to 25 MB, an account has room for 20 editions and 1 GB, and you print one thing at a time. Without an account, the number of prints is limited. We may change these limits, and change or stop Printopoly.

## Your photos and texts

What you upload or type stays yours. We only use it to keep your edition and make your PDFs.

You are responsible for what you upload:

- photos of people only if they agree;
- no photos or texts of others without their permission;
- nothing that breaks the law;
- no play money that looks like real banknotes.

An edition is for you and your friends. If you want to sell it, work out for yourself which rights are involved.

## Not affiliated with Hasbro

Printopoly is an independent project and is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Hasbro. Monopoly® is a trademark of Hasbro. We only mention that name to describe what you can use Printopoly for.

We write the default texts of Printopoly ourselves. The board, the cards and the money are an original design.

## Reporting something

Is there, for example, a photo of you on Printopoly that you did not agree to? Mail abuse@printopoly.nl with what is wrong and where it is. If the report is correct, we take it down.

## Applicable law

Dutch law applies. A dispute goes to the competent court in the Netherlands.
