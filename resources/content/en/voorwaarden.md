# Terms of use

These terms apply to your use of printopoly.nl. Printopoly is a free hobby project. What you make and print, you make and print yourself.

Printopoly is free. You only pay the print shop or your own ink and paper. We do our best to make the PDFs correct, but we cannot guarantee that a print fits your board exactly, that colours come out as they look on your screen, or that the site is always available. Print the test sheet first and check one corner space on your board before you order everything.

What you order from a print shop or print on your own printer is at your own cost.

As far as the law allows, we are not liable for damage from using Printopoly, such as a wrongly printed board or lost editions. Keep important editions in a second place, for example by logging in.

## Limits

To prevent abuse there are fixed limits: photos up to 25 MB, at most 20 editions and 1 GB per account, and one print job at a time. Without an account, the number of print jobs per IP address is limited. We may change these limits.

We may change or stop Printopoly. We delete an account that has not been used for 24 months, with an e-mail 30 days before. See the privacy statement.

What you upload or type stays yours. You only give us permission to store it and use it to make your PDFs.

You are responsible for what you upload. You only put photos of people in your edition if they agree. You do not upload photos or texts that others hold the rights to unless you have permission, and nothing that breaks the law. You do not make play money that looks like real banknotes.

An edition is meant for you and your friends. If you want to sell it or distribute it widely, work out for yourself which rights are involved.

## Reporting something that is not allowed

If you see something on Printopoly that you think is not allowed, such as a photo of yourself that you did not consent to, or an infringement of your rights, e-mail abuse@printopoly.nl with the reason and as much information as you can give us to find it. We look at every report. If the report is correct, we remove the content. Uploads are private, so we can often only see what is there with your help.
