# Rules booklet

The booklet explains the rules in its own words, with the name, theme and prices of your edition. It has 16 A5 pages. If you chose house rules, they are on page 14.

## Print-shop set

Have it printed on 120 to 170 gsm and stapled as an A5 booklet. Each page is a page of its own at 154 × 216 mm, with 3 mm bleed, in booklet order. The print shop imposes them itself.

Quantity: 16 pages.

## Home-print set

You get 4 sheets of A4 in landscape, with two pages on each side in booklet order.

1. Print all sheets on both sides, flipping on the short edge. If your print menu has no such option, print all fronts first and put the stack back in the tray the other way up.
2. Put the sheets in order: the sheet with the front side (pages 16 and 1) goes on top.
3. Fold the stack in half and press the fold flat.
4. Put two staples in the fold.

You can skip this part: the rules from your own box still work.
