# Invitation

An A6 card (105 × 148 mm) with a small picture of your board, the name of the edition and your text: date, place and what everyone brings. Leave the text empty and there are dotted lines to fill in by hand.

You also get a PNG 1080 pixels wide, to send by chat or email.

You choose how many invitations you need on the print page, at the invitation. An A6 card fits through any letterbox without an envelope, so you can hand them out along the street.

## Print-shop set

Order as an A6 card on 300 gsm, with 3 mm bleed. It is 1 card per page.

{if:!guide}Quantity: {invitations} copies of the same card.

## Home-print set

You get 1 sheet of A4 with 4 cards. Print on 160 to 200 gsm paper and cut along the dashed lines in the middle. The outer edge of the paper is the edge of the cards, so you do not trim it.

{if:!guide}Print the sheet {invitation_sheets} times for {invitations} invitations.

The card has a white border: your printer does not print to the edge of the paper, and the border keeps that from showing.

You can skip this part.
