# Test sheet

The test sheet shows whether your printer prints at the right size. Do this before you order or print anything else.

## Print-shop set

1. Print this sheet yourself, at home or at a copy shop. Do not order it with the print shop.
2. Choose A4 and "actual size" (sometimes "100%" or "no scaling"). Never choose "fit to page".
3. Measure the 100 mm ruler. Lay a bank card on the outline: it should fit exactly.
4. Cut out the corner space and the regular space and lay them on your board. If they fit, you can order the rest.

## Home-print set

1. Print this sheet on A4 at actual size, never on "fit to page".
2. Measure the 100 mm ruler. Lay a bank card on the outline: it should fit exactly.
3. Lay the corner space and the regular space on your board. If they fit, you can print the rest.

## It does not fit?

If the ruler is shorter or longer than 100 mm, your printer is scaling. Look in the print dialog for "scale", "fit" or "shrink to page" and turn it off. If the ruler is right but the space does not fit your board, enter the measurements of your board in the edition settings and print the test sheet again.
