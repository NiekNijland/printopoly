# Houses and hotels

These are 32 houses and 12 hotels to fold yourself, in the colours of your edition. A house is about 12 mm wide, a hotel 24 mm. If you chose a photo or emblem, it sits small on the front.

## Print-shop set and home-print set

Both get the same 3 sheets of A4: 2 sheets of houses and 1 sheet of hotels. You cut and fold them yourself, so you can print them at home or have a copy shop print them.

Print on 160 to 200 gsm paper. Thinner paper is hard to fold, and thicker card gets too stiff at this small size.

1. Cut along the solid line.
2. Fold along the dashed lines. Score the folds first with a ruler and an empty ballpoint.
3. Put glue on the grey tabs and stick the walls and the roof together. On a house, glue the sides first and then the two gable ends.
4. Let it dry for a moment.

You can skip this part: use the houses and hotels from your own box.
