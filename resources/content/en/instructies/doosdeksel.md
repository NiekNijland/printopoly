# Box lid

The box lid is a sticker of {lid_w} × {lid_h} mm, the size from the settings of your edition. You stick it onto the lid of your box.

## Print-shop set

Order as a custom sticker, matte.

Quantity: 1 piece of {lid_w} × {lid_h} mm. The PDF has 3 mm of extra picture around it, so no white edge is left after cutting. Upload the PDF as it is; the order sheet in the ZIP has the exact details for the print shop.

1. Degrease the lid and lay the sticker on it dry.
2. Pull off a strip of backing paper along one edge and stick that edge down.
3. Press with a bank card, from that edge to the other side, and peel off the backing slowly.

## Home-print set

Print on A4 sticker sheets, matte, at actual size.
{if:lid_pieces}The lid is larger than A4, so it comes in {lid_pieces} pieces that overlap by 1 cm.

1. Cut with a knife along a ruler, on the crop marks.
2. Degrease the lid and lay the sticker on it dry.
3. Pull off a strip of backing paper along one edge and stick that edge down.
4. Press with a bank card, from that edge to the other side, and peel off the backing slowly.

{if:lid_pieces}With more pieces, stick piece 1 down first like this. Then lay the next piece on it with its edge right on the dashed line, so the picture runs on.

You can skip this part: your own box stays as it is.
