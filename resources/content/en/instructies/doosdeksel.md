# Box lid

The box lid is a sticker at the size from the board profile of your edition, about 267 × 267 mm. You stick it onto the lid of your box.

## Print-shop set

Order as custom-size vinyl sticker, matte.

Quantity: 1 piece, about 267 × 267 mm, with 3 mm bleed. Upload the PDF as it is.

1. Degrease the lid and lay the sticker on it dry.
2. Pull off a strip of backing paper along one edge and stick that edge down.
3. Press with a bank card, from that edge to the other side, and peel off the backing slowly.

## Home-print set

Print on a sticker sheet, matte, at actual size. If the lid fits on A4, use A4. If it is larger, use A3. The other steps are the same as above. Cut with a knife along a ruler.

You can skip this part: your own box stays as it is.
