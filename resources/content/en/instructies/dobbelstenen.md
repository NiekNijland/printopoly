# Dice

One A4 sheet with the nets of two 24 mm dice, with the pips on them. If you chose a photo, it sits on every face, under the pips. Opposite faces add up to seven.

## Print-shop set and home-print set

Both get the same sheet. Print on 160 to 200 gsm paper.

1. Cut along the solid line.
2. Fold along the dashed lines and press the folds sharp.
3. Put glue on the grey tabs and close the die. The last face is easiest to close with tweezers or a pencil.
4. Want a heavier die? Put a small ball of paper inside before you close it.

You can skip this part: use the dice from your own box.
