# Board

You stick the board onto the board you already own. No board? Then stick it onto cardboard.

## Print-shop set

Order as custom-size vinyl sticker, white, matte laminated, with repositionable adhesive.

Quantity: {panels} pieces, each {panel_w} × {panel_h} mm, with 3 mm bleed on top ({bleed_w} × {bleed_h} mm). Upload each PDF as it is. The crop marks sit in the margin of the PDF, outside the bleed.

Sticking it on:

1. Degrease the board and fit each panel dry first.
2. Lay panel A against the outer corner and pull off 5 cm of backing paper.
3. Press down with a bank card, from the middle outward, and peel off the rest slowly.
4. Do the same with the other panels. The panels stop 1 mm before the fold.
5. Fold the board and check that the fold is free.

## Home-print set

Print on full A4 sticker sheets, matte, for your type of printer (inkjet or laser), at actual size. Quantity: {sheets} sheets. Each space is a separate sticker, 0.5 mm smaller than the space. The centre comes in four pieces.

Sticking it on:

1. Degrease the board.
2. Cut the stickers out with a knife along a ruler. Lay them on their spots without peeling.
3. Start at {start}. Pull off 3 cm of backing, stick down the edge and press with a bank card.
4. Work along the edge and finish with the four centre pieces along the fold lines.
5. Fold the board and check that the fold is free.

Prick an air bubble with a pin and smooth it flat.

### Without sticker paper or without a board

Print the same {sheets} sheets on plain paper of 90 to 120 gsm and glue the spaces onto sturdy cardboard.

1. Cut a piece of cardboard of {outer} × {outer} mm, from a moving box or a sheet of greyboard.
2. Cut out the spaces, the same way as the stickers.
3. Cover the back with a glue stick or spray glue and lay the spaces in place, starting with {start} in a corner.
4. Press each space down with a bank card and let it dry for an hour under a few books.

Want to fold the board? Cut the cardboard through the middle and tape the two halves together on the back with wide tape.
