# Play money

This set has {notes} notes, enough for {players} players: {split}. Everyone starts with {start_money}.

You choose more or less money on the print page, under Play money: for 2 to 4 players, for 5 to 8 players or the full bank.

## Print-shop set

Have the money printed at a copy shop on 90 to 120 gsm paper and cut on the crop marks. It is on SRA3, a sheet a little larger than A3. Every note has its own border, so there is no extra picture to cut away.

Quantity: {sheets} sheets of SRA3, with crop marks. Each sheet has a label with its denominations, so you know which notes are on it. Print at actual size and ask the shop not to scale.

## Home-print set

Print on plain A4 paper of 90 to 120 gsm, at actual size.

Quantity: {sheets} sheets of A4, with crop marks.

1. First cut the sheets into strips along the crop marks, with a knife or a paper trimmer.
2. Then cut each strip into notes.
3. Sort by denomination and bundle each pile with a rubber band.

You can skip this part: the money from your own box stays usable.
