# Play money

The game has 210 notes: 30 of each denomination (1, 5, 10, 20, 50, 100 and 500). With the starting money of 1500 per player, that is enough for a normal game.

## Print-shop set

Have the money printed at a copy shop on 90 to 120 gsm paper and cut on the crop marks. Choose SRA3 or A3.

Quantity: 15 sheets of SRA3, with crop marks. Each sheet has a label with its denomination, so you know which notes are on it. Print at actual size and ask the shop not to scale.

## Home-print set

Print on plain A4 paper of 90 to 120 gsm, at actual size.

Quantity: 35 sheets of A4, with crop marks.

1. First cut the sheets into strips along the crop marks, with a knife or a paper trimmer.
2. Then cut each strip into notes.
3. Sort by denomination and bundle each pile with a rubber band.

You can skip this part: the money from your own box stays usable.
