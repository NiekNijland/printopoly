## Print-shop set

Order as a card set with a custom design per card, on sturdy 350 gsm matte card, with rounded corners.

Quantity: {cards_all} cards, each with its own front and back.

Upload the PDF as it is. Each card is on its own page, with 3 mm of extra picture all round, front and back alternating. Do not change the page order and do not choose "merge duplex". Choose rounded corners if the print shop offers them. The shop does the cutting.

## Home-print set

Print on both sides of 160 to 200 gsm matte paper, at actual size. The PDF has {pages} pages: first a test page for front and back (2 pages), then {sheets} sheets of A4 portrait with 9 cards each. Each sheet is a page of fronts followed by a page of backs, mirrored so every back lands exactly behind its front.

1. Print only pages 1 and 2, on both sides, flipping on the long edge. The print dialog may call it "Long edge", "Long-edge binding" or "Book".
2. Hold the sheet up to the light. If the crosses on the front and back sit on top of each other, your printer is set up right. If not, read on the small scales how many millimetres they are off. Up to 1.5 mm will not show, because the backs run on that far.
3. If the text on the back is upside down, the printer flipped on the short edge. Choose the long edge and print the test again.
4. Then print pages 3 to {pages} the same way.
5. Cut the cards out with a knife along a ruler. The crop marks are in the margin of the sheet: lay the ruler across two marks facing each other.

Does your printer not print on both sides by itself? Then print the odd pages first, put the stack back in the tray turned over as your printer manual describes, and print the even pages. Try it with the test page first, so you know the stack is the right way round.
