# Cards and deeds

You print 16 Chance cards, 16 Community Chest cards and 28 title deeds new, front and back: 60 cards of 63 × 88 mm. You no longer need your old cards.

## Print-shop set

Order as a card set with a custom design per card, 350 gsm matte, rounded corners.

Quantity: 60 cards, each with its own front and back.

Upload the PDF as it is. Each card is on its own page at final size with 3 mm bleed, front and back alternating. Do not change the page order and do not choose "merge duplex". Choose rounded corners if the print shop offers them. The shop does the cutting.

## Home-print set

Print on 160 to 200 gsm matte paper, at actual size. Quantity: 15 sheets of A4 landscape, 4 cards per sheet.

1. Each card is a fold card: front and back sit side by side.
2. Cut the cards out with a knife along a ruler, on the crop marks.
3. Fold along the dotted line.
4. Glue the two halves together and let them dry under a book.

Do not print on both sides of the paper: that shifts on almost every home printer.
