# Questions

## What does it cost?

Printopoly is free. You only pay the print shop or your own ink and paper.

## Do I need an account?

No. Without an account your browser keeps everything you make. If you clear your browser data, it is gone, and you will not see it on another computer. If you log in with Google, your work is also in your account and you see it on every device.

## Which board does the sticker fit?

By default, a modern square board of about 50 × 50 cm. Print the test sheet on A4 at actual size and lay the corner space and the regular space on your board. If they fit, the rest fits too. If they do not, enter the measurements of your board in the settings of your edition.

## Do I have to make everything?

No. Only the board is required. You can take the cards, deeds, play money, pawns and box lid from your own box. You tick what goes in your set on the print page. If you change the street names, you do need new title deeds.

## Print shop or home printing?

The print shop gives the best result: the board as four large stickers you can still move while you stick them on, and sturdy playing cards. At home you print everything on A4 and spend longer cutting and sticking, because each space is a separate sticker and you print the cards on both sides. You can also mix, for example have the board printed and print the money at home.

## May I use photos of my friends?

Yes, if they agree. Only you can see your photos. For the PDFs they go to our server, and we delete them there after 7 days.

## Safari says my edition is wiped after 7 days. Is that right?

Yes. Safari clears the storage of a site you have not opened for 7 days. If you work in Safari or on an iPhone without an account, your edition may be gone after a week. Log in with Google to prevent that.

## Can I make more than one edition?

Yes, as many as you like. You find them under My editions. With an account you can keep up to 20.

## Can I change prices and rents?

Yes, up to 20% above or below the standard amount, so the game stays playable. That goes for every amount, including the salary at Start and the amounts on the cards. Rent and mortgage do not follow the price, you set them separately. An extra Chance or Community Chest card can have any amount up to 500.

## What if a photo is too small or text does not fit?

You see it at the space itself in the editor. The top of the print page lists everything under Check before you print, with a button to the spot where you fix it. You can always print.

## Does it work on my phone?

Yes. You tap a space and edit it at full size. You can share or save the PDFs from your phone.

## Is Printopoly part of Hasbro?

No. Printopoly is an independent project and has nothing to do with Hasbro. Monopoly® is a trademark of Hasbro.

## What do I need to print at home?

A printer, A4 sticker paper for the board, paper of 160 to 200 gsm for the cards, a sharp craft knife, a metal ruler and a cutting mat. For the cards a printer that prints on both sides helps. Print at actual size, so without "fit to page". The print guide explains everything. Plan on an evening of cutting and sticking.
